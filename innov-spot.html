Innovation Spotlight

Event Description

Paper presentation event where students gather to share their innovative ideas and research findings.

Participation Criteria

  • 1

    Eligibility

    The competition is open to all college students from various disciplines.

  • 2

    Team Size

    A team can have maximum of 2 members. Individual participation is also allowed.

  • 3

    Topic

    Papers can be in any engineering related topic.

General Rules

  • 1

    Paper Submission

    Participants must submit their papers in the specified format and within the given deadline.

  • 2

    Originality

    Papers must be original and not previously published or presented.

  • 3

    Content

    Papers should be relevant to the theme or topic of the event.

  • 4

    Language

    Papers should be written in English.

  • 5

    Time Limit

    Presentations should be within the specified time limit (5-7 minutes).

Paper Format

  • 1

    Length

    Papers should be within the specified limit (4-8 pages).

  • 2

    Font

    Use a standard font (Times New Roman).

Judging Criteria

  • 1

    Content

    Relevance, originality, and depth of content.

  • 2

    Presentation

    Clarity, organization, and effectiveness of the presentation.

  • 3

    Q&A

    Ability to answer questions confidently and accurately.

Registration and Participation

  • 1

    Registration

    Participants must register in advance and provide required details.

  • 2

    Participation

    Participants are expected to attend the event and present their papers.

Other Rules

  • 1

    Plagiarism

    Plagiarism will not be tolerated and may result in disqualification.

  • 2

    Decision

    Judges' decisions are final and binding.
