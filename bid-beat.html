🎶 Bid & Beat 🎶

The Ultimate Musical Bidding Showdown — Sing • Bid • Win ✨

🎤 How to Play

🌟 Objective: Compete in teams to bid and sing songs featuring the named actor — the highest bidder must perform all their claimed songs correctly to win points!

🧑‍🤝‍🧑 Form Teams: 2 members per team.

📣 Actor Announced: The host reveals an actor (e.g., “Shah Rukh Khan”).

🙋‍♀ Start Bidding: Teams quietly decide and bid how many songs they can sing. Example: “We bid 3!”

📈 Bidding War: Continue until one team wins the bid.

🏆 Win the Bid: The winning team must sing all songs they claimed — with movie names.

🎵 Beat the Bid: Each correct song = points! Fail and the next team can steal.

🔁 Next Round: New actor, new energy!

📜 Rules & Details

  • Valid Songs ✅ — Must be from movies featuring the given actor (cameos count).
  • No Repeats 🚫 — Same song can’t be reused in a round.
  • No Phones 📵 — Devices banned during play. Violation = -10 points.
  • Singing Requirement 🎙 — Minimum 2–3 lines or chorus. No humming.
  • Time Limits ⏳ — 10s for bidding, 30s per song performance.
  • Judge’s Call 🧑‍⚖ — Host’s decision is final.
  • Winning 🏅 — Highest total points = “Bid & Beat” Champion!

🕓 Event Duration

Total: 45–60 minutes

Rounds: 5–6

Each Round: ~5 minutes (bidding + performance)
