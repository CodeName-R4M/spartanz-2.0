Quizto Bash

Event Description

Quizto Bash is a two-round battle of wits and creativity! Round 1 tests your knowledge in a thrilling team quiz, while Round 2 challenges your design skills to craft innovative UI prototypes. Are you ready to prove your mettle?

Round 1: INNOQUIZ

  • 1

    Team Size

    Each team must consist of 2 members.

  • 2

    Punctuality

    Teams must report 10 minutes prior to the event start time.

  • 3

    Tie-Breaker

    In case of a tie, buzzer or tie-breaker questions will decide the winners.

  • 4

    Final Decision

    The decision of the Quiz Masters and Judges is final and binding.

  • 5

    Malpractice

    Any form of malpractice or misconduct will result in immediate disqualification.

  • 6

    Organizer Rights

    Organizers reserve the right to modify the rules or structure if required.

Round 2: UINOVA

  • ✔

    Team Size

    1-2 members

  • ✔

    Design Format

    Figma (Must submit a prototype)

  • ✔

    Presentation

    Must include UI design and usability considerations

  • ✔

    Originality

    No templates or pre-made UI kits allowed—design must be original

  • ✔

    Disqualification

    Plagiarism, exceeding time limit, or inappropriate content

General Rules

  • ⚡

    Eligibility

    Open to all students (any year)

  • 🛡️

    Fair Play

    All participants must adhere to the spirit of fair competition
