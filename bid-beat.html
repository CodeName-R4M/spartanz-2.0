🎶 Bid & Beat 🎶

The Ultimate Musical Bidding Showdown — Sing • Bid • Win ✨

🎵 Event Description

A fun auction-style musical game where teams bid on how many songs they can sing based on picture clues. Bid high, sing fast, and score points to beat the competition!

👥 Team Requirements

  • 2–3 members per team
  • No mobile phones or external help allowed

🏆 Scoring

  • Successful bid → 10 points
  • Failed attempt → 0 points
  • Highest total score wins

📜 Rules & Details

  • ■ Duration
    • Total duration: 45–60 minutes
    • Each round: ~5 minutes (bidding + performance)
    • No. of rounds: 5–6
  • ■ Participants
    • Team size: 2–3 members per team
  • ■ Rules
    • Each round, an image-based clue will be shown (example: Hero + Music Director, Movie + Lyricist, Singer + Heroine, etc.).
    • Teams must bid how many songs they can sing that fit the scenario in 1 minute.
    • The highest bidder plays first.
    • If they complete their bid successfully → they earn points.
    • If they fail → the chance passes to the next highest bidder.
    • A song cannot be repeated in the same round.
    • Teams must start singing immediately when the timer begins.
    • Judge’s/Coordinator’s decision is final in validating the songs.
