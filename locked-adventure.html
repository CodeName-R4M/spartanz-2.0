Locked Adventures: The Infinity Escape

Game Overview

You are an Avenger on a top-secret mission guided by Nick Fury himself. Your task is to collect all six Infinity Stones hidden across four challenging rooms before Thanos arrives. Each room is packed with riddles, codes, and traps — and only the sharpest heroes can survive.

Team Requirements

  • 1

    Team Formation

    This mission allows a maximum of 2 players — your partner is your greatest strength. Communication, focus, and teamwork are the keys to survival.

  • ⏱️

    Time Limit

    Each room must be completed within 10 minutes. If time runs out, Thanos draws closer, and you'll have to restart that room.

  • 🔍

    Nick Fury's Guidance

    Nick Fury is your virtual guide and mentor. You'll receive 2 hints per room — use them wisely. Once both hints are used, no further help will be given.

Rooms & Stones

  • 1

    Room 1: Power Stone

    Capture 1 Stone – The Power Stone.

  • 2

    Room 2: Time & Space Stones

    Collect 2 Stones – The Time Stone and Space Stone.

  • 3

    Room 3: Mind, Reality & Soul Stones

    Secure 3 Stones – The Mind Stone, Reality Stone, and Soul Stone.

  • 4

    Room 4: Infinity Gauntlet Chamber

    Enter the Infinity Gauntlet Chamber, packed with riddles and traps. Solve every challenge to unlock the Gauntlet and claim ultimate power.

Competition Rules

  • 🧩

    Puzzle Rules

    Each puzzle, lock, and code is a step toward the next stone. Logic, patience, and teamwork are your best weapons.

  • ⚠️

    Penalties

    Every wrong answer or misuse of a hint costs 30 seconds from your timer. Stay calm. Think smart. Time is your true enemy.

  • 🏆

    Winning the Game

    After securing all six stones and unlocking the Infinity Gauntlet in Room 4, you must assemble them all before Thanos arrives. Victory saves the universe — failure means its fall.

  • 💀

    Failure Condition

    If Thanos reaches the Gauntlet before you do, your mission resets, and you must begin again from the first room.

Important Notes

  • 🚫

    No External Help

    No external help or internet searches allowed.

  • 🤝

    Teamwork

    Teamwork and focus are the heart of success.

  • 💪

    Perseverance

    Remember: heroes never quit, even when time runs out.

Final Message from Nick Fury

"The fate of the universe rests not in power, but in unity.
Two heroes. One mission. Endless courage.
Now go — and prove that even locked doors can't stop Avengers."
