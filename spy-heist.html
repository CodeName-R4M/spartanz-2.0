Spy Heist

Event Description

A high-paced cybersecurity capture-the-flag challenge where you'll hunt for flags, exploit vulnerabilities, and outsmart your opponents. Test your hacking skills in this intense competition!

Team Requirements

  • 1

    Team Size

    1-2 participants per team

  • 2

    Equipment

    Participants must bring their own laptops with necessary tools installed

  • 3

    Challenge Format

    40 challenges of varying difficulty levels

Scoring System

  • S

    Points Allocation

    Points awarded based on challenge difficulty - harder challenges yield more points

  • W

    Winners

    Top 3 teams with the highest points at the end of the event will be declared winners

Competition Rules

  • ⚠️

    Strict Prohibition

    Collaboration between teams and external help is strictly prohibited

  • 🚫

    Disqualification

    Any form of misconduct or cheating will result in immediate disqualification

  • 🔒

    Security

    All attacks must be confined to the designated competition environment

  • ⏱️

    Time Limit

    The event will run for a fixed duration - all flags must be captured within this timeframe
