Treasure Hunt

Event Description

A high-paced treasure hunt where teams will solve clues to find hidden objects across campus locations. Test your observation skills, teamwork, and speed in this intense competition!

Duration: 45 – 60 minutes

No. of Participants: Approximately 40 – 60 members

Team Formation: 3 – 4 members per team

Concept: Object finding using hidden clues across campus locations

Team Requirements

  • 1

    Team Size

    3-4 participants per team

  • 2

    Equipment

    Mobile phones are allowed for navigation and communication

  • 3

    Participation

    All team members must stay together throughout the hunt

Scoring System

  • S

    Points Allocation

    Each found object = 1 point

  • W

    Winners

    Top team with the highest points at the end of the event will be declared winners

📜 Rules & Guidelines

  • 1

    Clue Distribution

    Each team receives the same set of 10 clues at the start.

  • 2

    Time Limit

    Teams must find as many hidden objects as possible within the given time (45–60 mins).

  • 3

    Mobile Usage

    Mobile phones are allowed, but most clues require on-campus observation.

  • 4

    Object Submission

    Teams must submit collected objects to event volunteers immediately after finding them.

  • 5

    Winning Criteria

    Winner is decided based on the maximum number of objects collected.

  • 6

    Tie-Breaker I

    In case of a tie (teams collecting all 10 objects), the fastest team to return wins.

  • 7

    Tie-Breaker II

    If still tied, a quick tie-breaker task (quiz/physical challenge) will decide the winner.

🏆 Winner Selection

  • 1

    Primary Criteria

    Team with the maximum number of objects.

  • 2

    Secondary Criteria

    If Tie: Team that returns the fastest with all objects.

  • 3

    Final Criteria

    If Still Tie: Tie-breaker challenge (quiz or physical task).
